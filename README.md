# Madison's Room — Bookshelf Bed Planner

An interactive 3D planner for Madison's bedroom (Bedroom 2, 3.53 m × 2.82 m):
a double mattress on 100 cm IKEA LASTARE cupboards with a crawl tunnel underneath,
a walnut BILLY bookcase wall, a swing-arm TV on the chimney breast, and a desk by the window.

**Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any browser** — no install needed. Everything runs locally.

## What you can do

- **Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom** — walls fade automatically so you can always see in. Use the Top view button for a plan view.
- **Under-bed cupboards** — pick which LASTARE unit sits in each of the 7 slots and follow the IKEA ↗ links to the exact products from the order. Warnings appear if a layout uses more units than were delivered.
- **Look inside** — untick "Show platform & mattress" to see the bare cupboard layout (handy on build day).
- **TV swing** — the ▶ button animates the double-jointed arm folding out from the chimney breast.
- **Export card** — download the floor plan as a PNG, or copy/paste the settings text to save and share exact layouts.

## Room facts baked in

- Room 3.53 × 2.82 m; chimney breast 165 × 20 cm (94 cm recess either side) on the bottom wall
- Window (75 cm) + radiator on the right wall; door on the top wall, hinged to open toward the window
- IKEA order delivered: 2 × LASTARE 80 open, 2 × 60 with doors, 2 × 40 open, 1 × chest of 4 drawers,
  ALTARLIDEN shelves/rail, BILLY 200 × 237 combo (brown walnut), 2 × MOSSLANDA ledges
- Still needed: 18 mm plywood platform (~140 × 200 cm), double mattress (135 × 190), TV + articulating bracket

⚠ Safety: IKEA flags LASTARE as a tipping hazard — bolt frames together, screw the platform to every frame,
anchor wall-side frames and the BILLY run to the wall, and check ceiling height (BILLY is 237 cm tall).
